Privacy Notice

Effective November 13, 2026

San Fernando Valley Gujarati Association (SFVGA) uses this app to share community information, organize events, manage registrations and tickets, and connect members with organizers. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to sfvgabod@gmail.com.

Information we handle

  • Your verified phone number, name, membership information, and any email address or profile photo you choose to provide.
  • Family-member names and details you enter to register people for events, which may include age or dietary information.
  • Event registrations, attendee names, payer name, optional payment note, payment-review status, tickets, and check-in records.
  • Private messages you exchange with organizers about a registration.
  • Notification preferences and a device push token if you enable notifications.
  • Technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the app through Firebase and the device platform.

How we use and share it

We use this information to provide the app, communicate with members, organize events, review payments, issue and validate tickets, and protect the service. Authorized SFVGA organizers can see information needed for their assigned roles. Firebase services operated by Google process account, database, file, cloud-function, and notification data for the app. Your device platform may also process push notifications. An external payment link takes you to the payment provider chosen for that event; its own privacy practices apply there. SFVGA does not ask for bank credentials in this app.

Retention and your choices

When you delete your account, we remove your sign-in, profile, contact details, family list, notifications, and private registration messages; your tickets stop working. We keep attendee and payer names in active historical registration records for six months after the event, then remove those names. Limited payment, check-in, and security records may be kept for legitimate recordkeeping or legal obligations. Backup copies may contain earlier data while SFVGA finalizes its backup-retention policy. SFVGA's president will review this retention schedule. You can edit your displayed name and notification choices in your profile. For access, correction, or a copy of your information, email sfvgabod@gmail.com. For self-service account deletion, see Account deletion.

Updates

We may update this notice as the app changes. The current version will be available here with a new effective date. Contact sfvgabod@gmail.com with questions.
