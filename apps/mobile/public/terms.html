Each event may have its own price, capacity, registration deadline, and organizer instructions. For paid events, following an external payment link alone does not complete registration or guarantee tickets. Finish the in-app registration steps, then wait for the organizer to verify payment and release tickets. If there is a payment issue, use the app's registration messages to contact organizers. Any event-specific cancellation or refund instructions should be reviewed before paying; contact organizers if they are unclear. External payment services have their own terms.
