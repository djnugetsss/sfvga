Delete your account

Updated November 13, 2026

Sign in to SFVGA on this website or your phone, open Profile, then select Delete my account. You can confirm the request there without sending an email.

Open SFVGA Profile to delete my account →

Deletion removes your sign-in, profile, contact details, family list, notifications, and private registration messages. Tickets stop working immediately. SFVGA keeps attendee and payer names in active historical registration records for six months after the event, then removes those names. Limited payment, check-in, and security records may remain for recordkeeping or legal obligations. Backup copies may contain earlier data while SFVGA finalizes its backup-retention policy.

If you cannot sign in or need help with an unresolved payment, contact sfvgabod@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or bank details.
